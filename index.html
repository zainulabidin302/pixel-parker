HOW TO PLAY

Drive into the green bay and stop with the whole car inside the lines. Hold still for a moment and the level is done. Every bump costs health, and harder bumps cost more. Each level has a countdown, and harder levels give you less time.

◀ ▶Steer left and right ▲Gas REVBrake, then reverse FIREShoot back on fight levels 11 to 15

Levels 1 to 7 teach parking, 8 to 10 are very hard reverse parking, and 11 to 15 add tanks, a helicopter and robbers. On fight levels you escape by parking in the safe bay.

QUESTIONS

The game says my parking doesn't count.

Read the line under the lot. It tells you what is missing: get the whole car inside, straighten up, stop the car, or turn around. Levels 3, 8 and 10 must be parked nose out (reverse in). Level 4 is nose first.

I'm stuck on a level.

After 25 seconds, after a few bumps, or on your third try, a dotted yellow path and an arrow show where to drive next. A red REVERSE label means back into the bay.

There is no sound.

Tap SND at the top of the game to switch sound on, and turn up the volume. Sound starts after your first tap.

Where is my progress saved?

Your stars are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting the app removes them.

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 27 September 2026. Applies to the Pixel Parker app for iPhone.

Pixel Parker does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • The game runs offline and makes no network requests.
  • Your level stars are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere and are removed when you delete the app.
  • The robber laugh uses your iPhone's built-in speech voice on the device. No audio is recorded, and the microphone and camera are never used.

Children

Pixel Parker is suitable for children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
